# Chapter 23
## Ships and Covers

Ade made the argument at 7:40 in the evening, because he was not a coward and because the argument, if it was going to die, deserved to die in a full room rather than in a hallway where it could haunt.

Helen was on a speaker, not a screen, by her choice. Wen was on video from Singapore, where it was morning, a glass of something hot beside her, no visible impatience, which was not the same as patience. Soren sat in a chair he had not bothered to adjust, the creased figure in a folder now, as if putting it away could return it to August. Lukas was awake in Zurich and had, against Hanae's instruction, begun a draft. Hanae had not opened it. Elena had sent a deputy to listen and had told the deputy not to speak unless someone claimed a legal power the office didn't have.

"If we stop and he ships 3S," Ade said, "the market gets a frontier model with no retained trace, and the lab that still has a monitor looks like it flinched. Flincing has a cost. The cost is not my feelings. The cost is that the next instrument, whatever it is, gets built by the people who didn't flinch, inside a product that can't be read. I don't trust our monitor. I trust it more than silence. Shipping Witness under constraints, no partner queues, no widen, internal only, is the less bad deployment. Stopping outright is a mood with a good vocabulary."

He had promised not to say mood. Mira watched him hear himself say it, too late, the way you hear a glass after it leaves the hand.

Soren made a small, unwilling sound, agreement torn out of him by a premise. "She's right about the ship. That's the only version of a restraint I can sign without lying to my own engineers about what we learned. Alders, out of Marenberg, if Lukas insists on being included, which he will, because inclusion is how he gets the Commission to return his calls. She didn't look at Wen when she said it. Wen said it anyway, cleaner, the way a person says a thing that has been waiting for the adjectives to leave.

"Then every fix you have named improves the performance of the test. None of them improves the thing the test was for."

Ade sat down. The sitting was the argument leaving his body. Mira had wanted this and did not enjoy it. There was a version of her, younger by a month, that would have enjoyed it.
